About JSC

Built on healthcare experience.

Founded in Asia in 2024, Jaap Suyk Consultancy was established to help a European manufacturer in healing, sealing, haemostasis and regeneration using autologous blood expand its business in the region.

Founder Jaap Suyk is Dutch in origin and has worked in the healthcare industry since 1986, including with B. Braun Medical and Fresenius Medical Care. JSC looks to work with ambitious manufacturers developing products that can contribute to healing and regeneration.

With a particular interest in autologous approaches using sources such as blood and adipose tissue, JSC combines its healthcare focus with regional connections across Asia Pacific.

Common questions

Planning healthcare expansion into APAC?

Answers to common questions from medical and healthcare manufacturers exploring the region.

What does JSC help healthcare companies with?

Jaap Suyk Consultancy supports healthcare manufacturers with sales and marketing, regulatory-affairs considerations, clinical-trial-related support and cadaver lab workshop organisation in Thailand, depending on the project.

Which companies is JSC suited to?

JSC is aimed at European and American healthcare and medical-product manufacturers exploring commercial opportunities in Asia Pacific, particularly companies looking for regional context and a partner to discuss their market-development priorities.

Which Asia-Pacific markets does JSC cover?

JSC's stated regional footprint includes Singapore, Australia, Bangladesh, Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam. Specific coverage and requirements should be confirmed for each project.

Can JSC support regulatory affairs and market entry?

JSC can help manufacturers consider regulatory affairs as part of their regional expansion planning. Regulatory requirements depend on the product and destination market, and project scope should be confirmed directly with JSC.

Does JSC organise cadaver lab workshops?

JSC can support the organisation of cadaver lab workshops in Thailand for relevant healthcare and surgical technologies. Contact JSC to discuss the intended audience, product and objectives.
